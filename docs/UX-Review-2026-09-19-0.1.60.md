# Bello Agent 0.1.60 — UX review

A pass over every screen of the app, rendered through the gallery harness in
light and dark, at the narrowest and widest geometry the window allows, read
once as a first-time user and once as someone who lives in it all day.

## How this was rendered

`PiAppTests/UIScreenshotTests.testRenderRedesignGallery` drives the real shell
against the synthetic loopback gateway. For this review it grew six scenes the
gallery never reached: a long chat with its turns open and folded, a running
turn with the live bar and a follow-up behind it, the sidebar with a topic and
marked rows, the sidebar at its minimum width, the error strip, and the window
at its smallest and at 1760 points.

```sh
export PI_APP_UI_SCREENSHOT_ROOT=/tmp/ux-gallery-before
export TEST_RUNNER_PI_APP_UI_SCREENSHOT_ROOT=/tmp/ux-gallery-before
"$LOCK" xcodebuild test-without-building -project PiApp.xcodeproj -scheme PiApp \
  -destination 'platform=macOS' -derivedDataPath "$PI_BUILD_ROOT/wt-ux" \
  CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO -only-testing:PiAppTests/UIScreenshotTests
```

## Ranked findings

Ranked by how often a user meets the thing, not by how bad it is. Everything in
tier 1 is on screen in almost every session; tier 3 is a sheet you open when
something has gone wrong.

### Tier 1 — the shell, every session

**1. The sidebar speaks the helper's language, not the reader's.**
`14b-sidebar-narrow-light.png`, `15-error-strip-light.png`. A chat that is
waiting its turn says `queued`; one whose helper is still opening says
`preparing`; one mid-compaction says `compacting`; one the reader stopped says
`paused`, in lower case, in blue. The live turn bar above the composer already
says "Working", "Waiting to start", "Compacting context" — so one chat has two
vocabularies, and the smaller, more often-read one is the internal one.
*Fixed.*

**2. "Queued · 1" over the follow-up panel.**
`13-running-with-queue-light.png`. The panel that holds what you typed while
the model was busy is headed with the word the wire uses. A reader wants to
know whether the message has been sent yet; "Queued" does not answer that.
*Fixed.*

**3. A marked row and the open chat look the same.**
`14-sidebar-topic-marks-light.png`. `PiSelectableRow` fills both `selected` and
`marked` rows with `piAccentSoft` and puts a one-point outline on the marked
ones. Its own comment says the opposite ("a marked row is outlined rather than
highlighted, so the single gliding selection stays unique"). With three or four
rows marked for an archive, the chat you are reading is no longer findable.
*Fixed.*

**4. The composer loses the model's name before it loses anything else.**
`16-window-narrow-light.png`, `02-side-light.png`. At 920 points, or in either
half of a split, `ModelSwitchPills` drops from full labels straight to three
unlabelled icons with chevrons. The single most important piece of state in the
composer — which model am I talking to, and is it overridden — becomes a hover.
*Fixed: the middle form now drops the effort label instead of keeping it, so
the order of loss is connection label → effort label → model label and the
model name is the last to go. It stays at three forms: a fourth `ViewThatFits`
candidate inside the one `runControls` already makes took
`ConversationPaneTests.testComposerBarFitsNarrowPanes` from 5.7 s to over
seven minutes, so the count is part of the fix.*

**5. The sidebar's rate figure is cut mid-number.**
`01-main-light.png`: `Latest 126397 to…`. `SidebarReportedRate` has a fixed
108-point slot and `SessionTimingMetric.rate.label` never abbreviates, so a
fast route produces a figure that looks broken rather than fast. *Fixed.*

**6. The footer's notice — the line that says what to do next — is capped at
300 points.** `14b-sidebar-narrow-light.png`, `16b-window-wide-light.png`:
`Run cancelled. Pending messages are paused; ins…`, truncated identically at
1440 and at 1760 points, because the cap is absolute. The instruction never
arrives; the rest is in a tooltip. *Fixed: it takes the width the bar has.*

**7. Everything that can be dragged says so only with the cursor.**
The sidebar/content boundary (`SidebarResizeHandle`) and the terminal's top edge
(`TerminalPanel`) are one-point hairlines that change `NSCursor` on hover.
Nothing in the picture says they move, and there is no keyboard path to either.
A first-time user does not discover that the sidebar is adjustable.
*Not fixed — `WorkspaceView.swift`; handed over below.*

**8. The archive button on a sidebar row is `opacity(hovering ? 1 : 0)`.**
It is the only way to archive a chat that is not a right-click menu, and macOS
has no keyboard route to a context menu. A keyboard-only user cannot archive,
pin, move to a topic or mark as read at all. *Not fixed — `WorkspaceView.swift`
and the command menu; handed over below.*

**9. Five unlabelled icons in the corner of the sidebar.**
`01-main-light.png`, bottom left: a chart, a ladybug, a book, a crossed-out
eye, a gear. Four of them open major surfaces (the usage report, the request
inspector, skills/MCP, settings) and the fifth toggles whether background
sessions are listed. Their meaning is in `help` only. The ladybug in particular
does not read as "inspect the HTTP this app sent".

**10. `Effort · default`, `Effort · model`.**
The reasoning pill in the composer names the wire parameter. "Effort · model"
means "let the model decide"; nothing on screen says so, and the pill lights up
orange as though something were overridden.

### Tier 2 — the sheets, most days

**11. The loudest control in Settings is Delete Connection.**
`05-profiles-light.png`. The footer runs Delete (filled danger pill) → Test →
status → Save (primary). The action almost every visit ends with is the
quietest thing there, and a destructive action that has not destroyed anything
yet — it only opens a question — is the most eye-catching. *Fixed: asking is
quiet, the confirmation stays loud.*

**12. `Vault revision 1. No connection test was sent.`**
`05-profiles-light.png` footer, and the sheet subtitle "…share one versioned
Keychain item owned by the signed app." Two lines of implementation detail in
the first place a reader looks. *Fixed.*

**13. Fetch, pull and push are three unlabelled arrows.**
`10-changes-light.png`. One of them sends your commits to a shared remote and
it looks exactly like the two that only read. *Fixed: named where the toolbar
has room, icons with counts when it does not.*

**14. A disabled Commit does not say what is missing.**
`10-changes-light.png`: "60 of 60 files" beside a greyed Commit. Onboarding
gets this right ("Enter your gateway's URL to continue"); the commit box does
not. *Fixed.*

**15. Two similar ticks with different meanings.**
Each file row in Changes carries a checkbox (include in the commit) and a `+`
(stage in git). Same size, same row, no labels, different models of what a
commit is. Left alone: resolving it is a design decision about whether the
panel has a staging area at all.

**16. `explicitOnly`, `UTF-16 offset 0`, `attempt 0`, `of 0 bytes`.**
`06-resources-light.png`, `04-inspector-light.png`. A skill's policy badge
prints the wire enum; the skill body's pager names a text encoding; every
ordinary request is labelled "attempt 0"; and the inspector's Overview tab
shows "Offset 0 of 0 bytes" under a screen full of JSON. *All four fixed.*

**17. The report's biggest figure is `$0.0101375 USD`.**
`03-report-light.png`. Seven decimals in 20-point type; the digits that matter
are the hardest to find. The exact figure is evidence and belongs in the
caption and the per-request rows, which already carry it. *Fixed.*

**18. "Requests per bucket".**
`03-report-light.png`. The query's word for its grouping, in the chart's
subtitle. *Fixed.*

**19. The chart's last x-axis label is clipped to "S…" (to "S" at 920 points).**
`03-report-light.png`, `03c-report-compact-light.png`. *Fixed.*

**20. A tab label breaks across two lines inside its own pill.**
`03c-report-compact-light.png`: the Activity control reads "Request / s".
`PiTabs` let its `Text` wrap. It is used in Settings, Changes, the diff viewer,
the inspector and the report, so this can happen anywhere. *Fixed.*

**21. Two primary buttons on the search sheet.**
`07-search-light.png`: a filled orange "Search" beside the field and a filled
orange "Copy Conversation" in the footer. One surface, one primary.

**22. "8 matches on this page" before anything has been searched.**
`07-search-light.png`. With an empty query the sheet claims matches it has not
found, and one of the listed rows (row 2) is blank with no explanation.

**23. Session info shows the same measurement twice, in jargon, without units.**
`11-session-info-light.png`: `LATEST TOK/S 222.3` beside `OUTPUT TOK/S 280.6`.
Two tiles of equal weight whose difference (latest request versus session
average) is only in the caption, and whose values carry no unit. The charts
below have no unit on their y-axes either.

**24. The terminal does not read as a terminal.**
`01b-terminal-light.png`. Its canvas was `piSurfaceSunken`, two per cent away
from the transcript above it, so the panel looked like more conversation in a
monospaced font. *Fixed: it has a canvas of its own, still cream.*

### Tier 3 — first run, error paths, edges

**25. First launch has two competing calls to action.**
`00-onboarding-light.png`. The wizard says "connect your LiteLLM gateway"
(step 1 of 3) while the live sidebar beside it says "Add a project to start
chatting" with its own button — which is step 3. The sidebar's five tool icons
are also live before anything is configured.

**26. A dead row in the first screen a new user sees.**
`00-onboarding-light.png`: `API / Responses`, a label and a value that cannot
be changed and that means nothing to someone who has not read the docs. Three
of the five fields on step 1 are optional or fixed.

**27. The error strip is weaker in dark than in light.**
`15-error-strip-light.png` vs `15-error-strip-dark.png`. The severity is
carried by `piDanger.opacity(0.35)` on a hairline; on `#2E2925` that is almost
invisible and the strip reads as an ordinary card with a red triangle. Its
three actions ("More", "Copy", "Dismiss") are ghost buttons that read as static
labels, and the strip covers the transcript line underneath rather than moving
it.

**28. The side pane is exactly half the window and cannot be dragged.**
`02-side-light.png`. There is a hairline between the two panes; a reader will
try it. Both panes also carry a full metrics footer, so every figure appears
twice, competing with itself.

**29. Project headers and topic headers compact differently.**
`14b-sidebar-narrow-light.png`. At 200 points the project header correctly
drops its changes and new-chat buttons and keeps only "…", while the topic
header directly under it keeps its count, its `+` and its "…" and squeezes the
topic's name to "Payme…". Two rows, two rules.

**30. Empty states that do not say what to do.**
`08-workspaces-light.png` shows "Select a project or create one." — good. But
`No chats yet` under a project in the sidebar, `No skills match` in the
resources sheet and `No commits yet.` in Changes are statements with no next
step, in a place where the next step exists (the `+` beside the project header,
clearing the filter, making a commit).

**31. Capitalisation and separators are not uniform.**
Report status pills are lower case (`completed`, `miss`) while every other
badge in the app is sentence case (`Saved · Read-only`, `Primary`, `Current`).
Section headers are upper-case micro in some sheets (`CONNECTIONS · 2`,
`PROJECTS · 2`, `CHANGES · 60`) and sentence-case headings in others
(`Folders`, `Remove`, `Models`).

### Geometry

* **200 pt sidebar** — rows survive; titles truncate to eight characters
  ("Harden t…"), which is expected at that width. The selection bar drops to
  "2" plus two icons. Topic headers do not compact (finding 29). The rate
  figure was the only thing actually broken, and is fixed.
* **~460–500 pt pane** (920 pt window, or either half of a split) — the model
  and connection pills lost their labels (fixed); the metrics footer goes to
  two rows and sits hard against the window's bottom edge with no separator;
  the report's tab pill broke its own word (fixed).
* **1200 pt+ pane** — the transcript keeps a sensible measure, but the
  composer, the queue panel and the metrics footer all run the full width
  while the text above them stops at about 1000 points, so the reading column
  and the controls no longer share an edge. The footer's notice stays clipped
  at 300 pt however much room there is (fixed).

### Light and dark

Both are finished. The two places they disagree: the error strip's severity
(finding 27) and the disabled send/queue button in the composer, which is
`piInkTertiary` on `piFillStrong` — readable on cream, nearly invisible on
`#2E2925`.

## Handed to the orchestrator

These need `WorkspaceView.swift`, `Transcript/**` or `Composer/NativeComposer.swift`
beyond a one-line string, so they are not in this branch.

**A. Make the two resize handles visible (finding 7).**
`WorkspaceView.swift`, `private struct SidebarResizeHandle`, and
`Workspaces/TerminalPanel.swift`, the `Rectangle().overlay { … }` at the top of
`TerminalPanel.body`. Both are a one-point hairline with a nine-point invisible
hit area. Give each a grip that appears on hover and a tint that stays: in the
handle's overlay, draw a 2 × 18 pt (or 18 × 2 pt) capsule of `Color.piHairlineStrong`
centred on the hairline, at `opacity(hovering ? 1 : 0.35)`, and widen the hairline
to `Color.piHairlineStrong` while dragging. *Why:* an affordance may be
strengthened by hover, never created by it. The keyboard path is (B).

**B. Give the sidebar width and the terminal height a keyboard path, and put
the row actions in the menus (findings 7 and 8).**
`Application/PiApp.swift` command menu: add "Widen Sidebar"/"Narrow Sidebar"
(⌃⌘→ / ⌃⌘←) writing the same `sidebarWidth` default the handle commits, and
"Archive Chat", "Pin Chat", "Move to Topic…" and "Mark as Read" to the existing
`newItem` group, acting on `commandModel.focusedSessionID ?? selectedID`. All
four already exist as model methods used by the context menu. *Why:* every
pointer path needs a keyboard path.

**C. The archive button must not be invisible (finding 8).**
`WorkspaceView.swift`, `ChatRowBody`: `.opacity(hovering ? 1 : 0)` →
`.opacity(hovering || confirmingArchive ? 1 : 0.28)`. At 0.28 it is a quiet
grey glyph that becomes solid under the pointer, and a keyboard user tabbing
through the row can see where focus is. *Why:* hover may add, never be the only
way.

**D. The error strip should not be quieter in dark, and should not cover the
transcript (finding 27).**
`WorkspaceView.swift`, `struct ErrorBanner`: the border is
`Color.piDanger.opacity(0.35)`; give the strip a `Color.piDanger.opacity(0.10)`
wash behind `piSurface` and raise the border to `0.55`, so the severity reads
at the same strength in both appearances. Then promote "Dismiss" from
`.piGhost` to `.piSecondaryCompact` so the three actions read as controls.
Separately, the strip is an `.overlay(alignment: .top)` on the whole window,
which both centres it over the sidebar and paints it over the first line of the
transcript; moving it to a `safeAreaInset(edge: .top)` on the content column
would let it push the reading position instead of hiding it.

**E. Two primaries on the search sheet (finding 21).**
`Inspector/ConversationContentView.swift` — in my area, but the choice of which
button is primary is a product decision: I would make "Search" a
`.piSecondaryCompact` (Return in the field already runs it) and leave
"Copy Conversation" as the one primary.

**F. The split pane should be draggable, or should not look draggable
(finding 28).** `WorkspaceView.swift` lays the side out as
`(region.size.width / 2).rounded(.down)`. Either give the boundary the same
handle as (A) with a stored fraction, or remove the hairline between the panes
so it does not invite a drag it cannot answer. The duplicated metrics footer in
the side pane is the same call: a side chat rarely needs its own context ring
and cost.

**G. "Effort" (finding 10).** `Workspaces/ModelCatalog.swift`,
`ThinkingLevel.pillLabel` — "Effort · default" → "Thinking · connection
default", "Effort · model" → "Thinking · model decides". One-line strings, but
`ModelSwitchTests` asserts on the level labels, so it wants to land with its
test in the same change.

**H. The sidebar's five corner icons (finding 9).** Either a one-word caption
under each at widths above 260 pt, or move the inspector and skills into the
"…" of the project header and leave three. Needs a decision about what the
sidebar footer is for.

## What was checked and found sound

* Every sheet closes on Escape (`PiSheet` carries a hidden cancel button);
  every sheet has a visible way out as well.
* The live turn bar's own vocabulary ("Working", "Waiting to start",
  "Compacting context", "Stopping") is already plain, and its Stop button is
  the only red thing on the screen while a turn runs.
* Every destructive action names what it destroys before doing it: discard
  ("Tracked files revert to HEAD and untracked files are deleted. Git keeps no
  copy"), delete a connection ("Its key leaves the Keychain item. 3 chats keep
  their history…"), remove a project ("Bello Agent forgets this project and its
  folder trust. Nothing on disk is deleted."), remove a topic ("Its chats stay
  in the project"), archive a chat (one click asks, a second confirms).
* Disabled primaries in onboarding and in Projects say why they are disabled.
* The tool-call group in the transcript has a visible chevron and a plain
  summary ("Read 1 file"), and it folds.
* Reduce Motion is honoured through `PiMotion`/`piAnimation` everywhere the
  gallery could reach.
* The markdown, code blocks and Unicode in the transcript render correctly in
  both appearances at every width tested.

## Not verified here

Pointer-only behaviour (hover timing, drag-and-drop between topics, the
rate popover's hover-then-pin), VoiceOver rotor order, a real gateway's error
bodies, and the appearance of the menu-bar item — none of which the window
capture can show.

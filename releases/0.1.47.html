Bello Agent 0.1.47

  • Retry request sends the model and reasoning effort the chat has selected at the moment you click it, so a model switched after a failure is what retries; cleared pills retry with the connection's own defaults.
  • The output budget is metadata only. It is never sent as a limit and never fails a turn; a reply runs to the model's own output ceiling from the catalog (clipped only to the room a nearly full context window leaves). The budget just sizes the reserve that decides when a chat compacts.
  • The Usage menu's model distribution stays readable with very long model ids: labels are cut in the middle and the bars and figures keep their columns.

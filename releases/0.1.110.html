Bello Agent 0.1.110

  • A side you closed no longer comes back at the next launch. While the app was still opening the chat you had open, which for a long chat could take seconds with its side already showing, closing the side was not recorded, and the next launch reopened it.
  • Quitting before the app had finished opening now also keeps the chat you opened and the side you closed.
  • If a side has been coming back, it opens once more after this update, because the saved record still names it. Close it once and it stays closed.

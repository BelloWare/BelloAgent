Bello Agent 0.1.29

  • Errors now appear inside the conversation where it stopped, instead of a fixed strip above it. A refused send shows there too.
  • Transient gateway failures (dropped streams, rate limits, 5xx, overload) are retried up to two more times before anything is reported, with the retry shown in the chat.
  • Long conversations load as you scroll up, and switching chats always shows the latest messages with earlier pages a scroll away.
  • A new chat or side is only created when you send its first message; empty ones vanish when you move on.
  • One window: the menu bar item and the Dock bring it forward instead of opening a duplicate.
  • The sidebar starts wider.
  • Turn totals (time, replies, tool calls, model versus tool time) now sit under every turn and count up while the turn is running; replies no longer carry their own time figures.

Bello Agent 0.1.70

  • A short, clear chime now announces completed chat tasks, including those finishing in the background.
  • Find the new completion sound toggle and Preview button in Settings → Notifications. Sound is enabled by default.
  • The cue waits for the whole task, including its tools, to finish. Opening old chats, utility requests, errors and stopped work stay quiet.
  • Sessions finishing together share one cue so sounds do not overlap or queue up.

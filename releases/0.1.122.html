Bello Agent 0.1.122

  • Smooth scrolling in long chats. Scrolling up or down a very long chat keeps your place: earlier and later messages are read in ahead of you, and the chat no longer jumps or stalls at the edge. Home or ⌘↑ goes to the first message in one press; End or ⌘↓ goes to the latest.
  • Search inside chats. The sidebar search now matches message text as well as titles. Each match shows a snippet, and opening it goes to that message. In an open chat, ⌘F shows a find bar: ⌘G and ⇧⌘G step through the matches. Search and Copy Conversation moves to ⌥⌘F.
  • Sorted by last activity. The sidebar always lists the most recently active chats first, with pinned chats on top. Rows stay put while your pointer is over the list. Drag-to-reorder has been removed.
  • Mark as Unread. Right-click a chat, or use File ▸ Mark as Unread, to keep its unread dot until you open it again.
  • Paused chats stay paused. A chat you paused, or one holding queued messages, still shows as paused after the app restarts.
  • Recently opened chats are tinted. The chat you have open has the strongest tint, and the few you opened before it are tinted less and less.
  • Draft marker. A pencil marks chats with a message you haven't sent yet.
  • Typing a commit message in the Changes panel is faster.
  • The queue's "Paused" label no longer breaks mid-word in a narrow chat.

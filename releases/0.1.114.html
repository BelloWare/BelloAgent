Bello Agent 0.1.114

  • Faster redraws. A change anywhere in the app no longer redraws the transcript, its live bar and the footer's figures: 3.2 ms per change instead of 5.8 ms. Switching back to one of your last three chats reuses its rows, so it is ready in 67 ms instead of 122 ms.
  • Chats are drawn once, where they stay.
    • Opening a chat no longer jumps after it is drawn: by up to thousands of points on a first open, or from its question to the end on a revisit.
    • An idle chat whose last turn is taller than the window opens at that turn's question again.
    • Switching away from a running chat no longer leaves its live bar or rows behind for a moment, and a reply's figures that arrive later no longer move rows.
    • A chat you left mid-reply opens as it is now.
  • Less memory with long chats. Older messages are read from the chat's file where they are instead of being held: after paging back or searching, the helper holds 6 to 12 MB more instead of 46 to 139 MB. The first older page is about 10% slower.
  • The app's temporary history index files are removed when it quits, and any left behind are removed at launch.
  • The Changes sheet (timings from a debug build):
    • opens without layout cycles, and draws its diff as a native table: from the whole diff to side by side in 24 to 35 ms instead of 209 to 230, a deep jump in 5 to 6 ms instead of 122 to 132, and a 400-file commit in 11 to 12 ms instead of 192 to 204;
    • shows all of 3,000 file chips in 68 ms instead of about 50 seconds;
    • lets you select text across diff lines, with Copy, Select All and Copy Path in the context menu;
    • redraws only what changed: a keystroke in the commit message takes 5 ms instead of 20 to 44.
  • Every sheet opens in a window of the app's own, and a closed sheet no longer keeps its views: closing Settings keeps 1.3 MB instead of 7.4 MB, Resources 0.8 MB instead of 7.3 MB, and Changes almost nothing instead of 20 to 40 MB.
  • The Session Inspector opens with less work (its Overview in 614 ms instead of 771 ms in a debug build), builds the ledger's rows as they scroll in, and loads a 5 MB request's tabs about twice as fast.
  • The archive is one switch, in the sidebar footer and in View ▸ Show/Hide Archived Chats. Archived chats are listed after each project's and topic's active chats, under “Archived · N”. The switch is remembered, and opening an archived chat turns it on. The per-project “Archive · N” views are gone.
  • Background requests have a page of their own: chat-title generation, title suggestions and webhook notifications, with filters, status, results, cost, and each request's prompt and reply, plus Open Source Chat and Inspect Requests. Open it with the sidebar footer button, View ▸ Background Requests (⇧⌘B) or the menu bar's running requests. Background chats no longer appear in the sidebar, and the eye toggle is gone. Records are kept, and those cut short by quitting are marked Interrupted.
  • Fixes: a title-suggestion or webhook request sent right after saving Settings could go out without the model catalog's output limit; and a fork or kept side opened just as its file was renamed could stay on “Preparing…”.

Bello Agent 0.1.30

  • The turn line under each answer now sums the turn's tokens and cost alongside its time, replies, tool calls and model versus tool split. Click it to see each request's usage and open its details.
  • Replies no longer repeat their usage line under themselves; the detail lives in the turn line.
  • A spinner turns while a reply is being worked on, while a turn is still running and while a failed request is being retried, and the retry says which attempt it is.
  • The reply line names only what was done; the "Did" label is gone.

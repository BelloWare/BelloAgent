Bello Agent 0.1.92

  • Summaries of very long chats are no longer held to 13,107 tokens. When a chat's history is too long for one summary request and is summarized in parts, each part now has a quarter of the model's window, or the model's own output limit if that is smaller, for its reasoning and summary.
  • This completes 0.1.91's removal of the summary cap. If you saw "Summary generation hit its 13107-token cap", that message came from 0.1.90 or earlier.

Bello Agent 0.1.43

  • Setting up a connection works in the order you fill it in. The API key sits right under the base URL, and the model picker lists models before the connection is saved: the included Bello catalog needs no key, and a gateway's own catalog uses the key you typed. Changing the base URL or catalog URL relists on the spot; a picker that cannot list says why.
  • Edits stay with their tab. Switching connections no longer discards what you typed; a dot marks a tab with unsaved edits, Save writes every edited tab, and Discard drops an unsaved one. A rename shows everywhere as soon as it is saved.
  • Saving no longer fails quietly because something else wrote the vault while Settings was open, and a save that does fail keeps your edits on their tab and says why in red.

Bello Agent 0.1.16

  • The Stop button now lives in the chat input, beside the send and queue controls.
  • Tool calls and results stay collapsed until you open them, including while a tool is running. Status remains visible.
  • Chat timing shows both the latest request's tokens per second and the session average, with history available on hover.
  • Failed responses show Error and a visible explanation. Deliberately paused or cancelled work remains distinct, and pending messages do not retry automatically.

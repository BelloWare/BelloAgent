  • Dragging a chat in the sidebar works now. Press a row and pull: a "N chats" image follows the pointer, the whole project or topic lights up as you pass over it, and the chat lands where you drop it. Before, the drag never started because the row's button took the press.
  • Folding a long turn's work (dozens of tool calls) is instant and clean. The fold used to arrive a beat late, rows painted over each other while the height caught up, and a turn could unfold itself as it streamed. The fold now happens in the same pass as the click, rows never draw outside their frame, and a folded turn stays folded.

Bello Agent 0.1.40

  • Opening a saved chat shows the question that started its last turn: the page no longer begins in the middle of a reply's tool calls, and when the last reply is taller than the window the chat opens at your question instead of at the bottom. Sending a message still jumps to the bottom.
  • Connections can be deleted from Settings. Their chats keep their history and ask for another connection; a connection whose chat is still working refuses until it stops. Renaming a connection keeps its key, chats and model cache.
  • Chat titles arrive as models actually write them: a reply with a label, quotes, a bullet or a second line is accepted, and a title request that fails says why in the chat's footer and the window's banner. Generate Title in the chat's menu asks again.
  • Faster: the terminal takes program output about six times faster, a streaming reply parses only the part still changing, and long replies render with less work per block.

Bello Agent 0.1.83

  • Always fetch final timing and request metrics when a fast turn finishes during the live refresh throttle.
  • Completed, failed and stopped turns no longer retain pending or still-running labels in their reports and copied info.
  • Request details clear the previous execution's payload when moving to another turn, including while loading or after a lookup fails.
  • The live monitor recovers from a disconnect even when the next successful poll contains no new events, and clears stale connection warnings when the last affected session is removed.

Bello Agent 0.1.33

  • A turn with a single reply now has one line instead of two near-duplicates: the reply's work, duration, model versus tool time, tokens, cost and model, labelled Turn.
  • The turn line no longer leaves an empty band under its text.
  • Conversations read turn by turn: more room before each of your messages, less between a message and its reply.
  • Code blocks show their language next to the copy button on hover; long prompts wrap at a readable width; the composer's keyboard hints are its placeholder and disappear as you type.
  • The app name and icon leave the top of the sidebar; New Chat sits on the Projects row. The Bello mark now greets you on an empty chat and pulses while a chat is being prepared.
  • Motion: new messages slide in, a finished turn glows briefly where it ended, details unfold smoothly, the live bar breathes while the AI works and shows when the turn started, copies confirm with a pop, the terminal slides in, and sidebar chats animate in and out. All of it respects Reduce Motion.

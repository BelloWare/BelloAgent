Bello Agent 0.1.133

  • Pasted images go out with your message. In 0.1.130, pressing Send right after pasting could send the text without the image, and some pastes flashed and attached nothing. Paste now takes its place in the draft before the clipboard is read, a Send pressed before the image is ready does nothing, every pasted image shows a thumbnail on its chip, and when an image can't be attached the composer says why.
  • Sent images show in the message. A message you sent with images shows them as small thumbnails under its text, in main chats and sides; click one to see it larger. The images are read from the chat's history only when they scroll into view.
  • Long chats load in larger pieces and stay fast. A turn's tool calls are grouped the same way however much of a long chat is loaded. A long chat opens with its latest rows first and fills in the rest right after, and reading back loads 1,000 rows at a time. A reply streaming into a long chat now costs about as much as in a short one.
  • Editing a message starts at once. Edit puts a message you can see in the composer straight away, even in very large chats, where it used to wait for the whole original to be read. Sending the edit no longer replays the whole chat (in a 259 MB chat, 10.6 s became 1.7 s). An edit you open during a run can be sent after the run, as long as only later messages changed.
  • Slash commands while editing. Editing an earlier or queued message offers the same slash list and skill chips as the composer. /side, /fork and /compact wait until you finish or cancel the edit, and /debug opens the Inspector.
  • Chat briefs. When a run ends, the chat's connection's mini model updates a one- or two-sentence brief and a few keywords for the chat, from its previous brief and what is new. Hover a chat in the sidebar to see its brief; Titles & details search finds chats by their briefs. Write Brief makes one for a chat that has none, Settings ▸ Chats & notifications can write briefs for all such chats and shows what briefs cost, and briefs can be turned off there.
  • Project filter in the Flat sidebar. Chips above the Flat list, and a row's project tag, show only one project's chats, newest first; All shows every chat again.
  • The sidebar stays at its top when a chat's activity moves the chat there, instead of scrolling down and hiding it. Scrolled down, the sidebar keeps your place, and a selected chat that moves stays in view.
  • A chat that has just finished opens quickly, with its finished rows shown at once instead of behind a cover, also while other chats are running.
  • Raising a chat's cost limit and pressing Resume works for any amount: a limit chosen as its editor closed could be lost before it reached the chat, so Resume was refused. After a raise, the line under the composer agrees with the card for the chat, its sides and sub-agents, and a limit that fails to save is tried again.
  • Switching chats beside the Agents, Diff and Files card is faster again: a new reply is laid out once at its real width, and buttons, tabs and pills no longer redraw when nothing about them changed.

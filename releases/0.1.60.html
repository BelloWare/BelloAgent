Bello Agent 0.1.60

  • Faster everywhere. A long chat opens in a fraction of a second instead of seconds, switching chats is instant, folding a turn with dozens of tool calls takes a couple of milliseconds, a side pane opens without a stall, scrolling a very long chat keeps up with the display, and a streamed token costs its own row rather than the whole page (the helper now sends what changed, about a hundredth of the bytes).
  • Transitions that feel right. Folding and unfolding, tool cards and reasoning move instead of snapping, the live bar slides into place, sidebar rows unfold from under their header, panels and the error strip arrive smoothly, and none of it moves the line you are reading. Reduce Motion turns it all off.
  • A clearer UI. Run states in plain words, marked rows that do not look like the open chat, visible grips on every draggable edge and a draggable split pane, keyboard shortcuts for sidebar width, archive, pin, move and fold, an error strip that pushes content instead of covering it, named git actions, readable rates and figures, and a review of every screen behind it.
  • Lighter and steadier. The app no longer keeps every chat you visited in memory, the sidebar rebuilds two rows instead of every row when something changes, launch shows your projects immediately, and polls stop for windows you cannot see.
  • Under the hood, the code is organised along its seams and the helper can no longer be crashed by a request.

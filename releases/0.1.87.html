Bello Agent 0.1.87

  • Your message appears the moment you press Return, even when the chat's helper had gone idle. The composer clears at once, and a send that fails puts your text back.
  • Skills show inline, as tokens at the start of the composer and as pills on the messages that used them. Hover for a preview, or click for the source, scope, version and whether the skill changed since you sent it.
  • Editing a message follows the new reply without "Load newer" strips, and older history loads as you scroll, with only a small spinner if it takes a moment.
  • The context meter counts the way pi does: the last reply's reported tokens plus an estimate for the messages since. It shows "pending" after a compaction, and compaction starts at pi's threshold.
  • Nothing you were doing is lost across quit or relaunch. Saved drafts are never erased, and side-chat text moves to its chat. Stop and Quit keeps the partial reply, your reading position holds however far back you were, and interrupted runs come back with their paused follow-ups.
  • A chat whose last record was cut off opens read-only with Recover Copy, and a moved project folder can be located again.
  • Polish: tool rows no longer show the system focus ring, "Ask in side chat" is a compact bar, and Session info, the report and the zoom remember how you left them.

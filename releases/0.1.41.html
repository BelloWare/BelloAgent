Bello Agent 0.1.41

  • Delete Connection works from the Settings footer itself: the button asks in place, says what the deletion touches (the key, the chats that keep their history, any run that will stop), and a run still going under the connection is stopped instead of blocking the deletion. The footer then says that the connection was deleted, or why it was not.
  • Motion, now that the whole app is native: the sidebar's selection highlight glides from chat to chat, the next chat rises into place while the last fades, tab strips slide their selected pill, a sheet's icon pops in as it lands, an empty chat's card builds up line by line, title suggestions arrive one after another, a tool row's glyph bounces once as the call finishes, footer figures roll to their new values, unread dots pop and the terminal springs open. Everything honours Reduce Motion.

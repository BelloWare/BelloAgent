Bello Agent 0.1.35

  • A calmer look with the same detail: your messages sit in a soft tint without an outline, a finished turn closes with a hairline and one quiet line of figures instead of a grey band, and the live bar, composer and cards cast shorter, softer shadows.
  • One motion language: every hover, fold and arrival shares the same easing and timing, so buttons, rows, chevrons and details respond alike everywhere. A settled turn warms its hairline for a moment rather than flashing.
  • Turn figures wrap between their dots in a narrow side pane instead of being cut off.
  • Footer metric icons step back so the numbers read first.
  • The cursor is where you are about to type: a new chat, a chat you select, a side you open or return to after closing one, and a message you edit all put the cursor in the right composer. Typing after a click on the transcript or a sidebar row goes into the composer instead of nowhere; text fields, the terminal and keyboard shortcuts are left alone, and Escape leaves the chat filter for the composer.
  • Nothing clips when a pane is narrow: the composer's connection, model and effort pills shorten to icons before the send button would be pushed off; the metrics footer drops rows it cannot fit; sidebar chat rows drop token and recency figures instead of cutting words; the empty-chat buttons wrap; turn figures wrap between their dots.

Bello Agent 0.1.107

  • A stopped turn says so once. The turn's card says "Stopped" and counts the request you stopped as stopped, not as "failed before it finished"; its advice now sits in the card's note, and the chip under the reply and the amber line under the card are gone.
  • A finished turn's times are rounded: "92 ms", "19.7s", "1m 05s" instead of three decimals of a millisecond. The Session Inspector still has each request's exact time.
  • Settings is in four sections, listed down its left side: Connections, Usage & capture, Chats & notifications (with the webhook), and App. Each shows only its own settings, one Save still saves them all, and Settings reopens at the section you used last.
  • Settings says the mini model also writes the webhook's parameters.
